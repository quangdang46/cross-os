# Safety: stop, reset, undo

- **PANIC STOP** (Safety page): stops CrossOS from affecting your computer
  instantly — keyboard shortcuts pass through normally. Your login item
  stays so you can re-enable.
- **Reset Everything**: removes the login item, turns off the Finder
  addition, cleans what CrossOS created, and checks nothing is left
  running. Only removes what CrossOS created — never your files.
- **Trial**: new additions run briefly first. Confirm on the Safety page,
  or they roll back by themselves.
- **Uninstall**: Reset Everything, then drag the app to Trash (macOS) or
  uninstall (Windows). System permission entries may remain until you
  remove them in System Settings.

What CrossOS created is listed on the Safety page, each with its own
undo button.
