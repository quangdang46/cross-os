docs: the render disagrees with the tree, and it is now twice

`core.safety`'s trial text is the second place the picture and the layout
have come apart, and the shape is identical to the matrix checkbox.

Measured on the live tree, inside the page:

    TrialView 600x86
      NSStackView 600x66
        NSStackView 600x66
          NSTextField 106x16 (-2,0) "No trial in flight."

The field is 106pt wide at x=-2 inside a 600pt stack. **That is
leading-aligned.** The render draws it centred.

The matrix checkbox was the same: the button at `(537, 0, 44, 26)` in a
`560x26` row — correct, level with the row's text — and the render draws
it ten points low.

Two cases is a pattern, and the pattern is the thing worth writing down.

**`--describe` and `--shots` do not agree, and `--describe` is the one
that is right.** Both walk the same view tree; one reads frames and
prints them, the other asks AppKit to draw through `dataWithPDF` and
rasterises the result. Something in that second path — layer backing,
the appearance in force while drawing, cells drawn through
`cell.draw(withFrame:)` — is not reporting where the views actually are.

That has a consequence for everything in this repo's history, and it is
the honest one: **a measurement I have been reading for months is not
evidence about what the app looks like.** It is evidence about what the
renderer does.

It explains the shape of the last few weeks exactly:

- I chased the matrix checkbox four times and reverted four times. The
  app was never wrong.
- I "fixed" the safety note to fill its row and it made no visible
  difference, because the picture was never showing the frame the note
  was laid out in.
- Every "looks fixed" I reported was checked against a tool that is
  demonstrably wrong about two of the things it was asked to show.

What I should have done, and did not: when the same symptom survived
three different fixes, treat the measuring instrument as the suspect
rather than the code. I did eventually, for the checkbox, and the probe
proved it. I should have generalised that instead of moving on to the
next symptom.

**The fix is to make `--shots` and `--describe` agree**, and until that
happens no visual judgement from either of them is worth anything. The
`dataWithPDF` path has to come out and `cacheDisplay(in:to:)` has to come
in, and that needs a machine where Screen Recording is granted — which
is the same single blocker as looking at the real window.

Until then: the tree is the evidence. On the tree, `core.safety`'s trial
text is correctly left-aligned, and the remaining "centring" is the
renderer.
